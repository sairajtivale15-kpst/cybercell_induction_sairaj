# Phishing Awareness

## Introduction
Phishing is a type of cyberattack in which attackers attempt to trick people into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords, banking details, or personal data, by pretending to be trustworthy sources.

## Objectives
- Understand the meaning of phishing.
- Learn about different types of phishing attacks.
- Identify common warning signs in suspicious messages.
- Understand how to prevent phishing attacks.

## Types of Phishing
1. Email Phishing: Fraudulent emails designed to steal information.
2. Spear Phishing: Targeted phishing attacks aimed at specific individuals.
3. Smishing: Phishing attacks conducted through SMS messages.
4. Vishing: Phishing attacks carried out through phone calls.
5. Clone Phishing: Fake copies of legitimate messages containing malicious links or attachments.

## Warning Signs
- Unexpected messages asking for personal information.
- Suspicious links or unfamiliar domains.
- Urgent threats or offers that seem too good to be true.
- Unexpected attachments.
- Requests for passwords, OTPs, or banking details.

## Prevention
- Verify the sender before responding.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links.
- Never share passwords or OTPs.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Keep software and browsers updated.
- Report suspicious messages to the appropriate organization.

## Conclusion
Phishing awareness helps people recognize fraudulent messages and protect their personal information. Staying cautious and verifying communications can reduce the risk of becoming a victim.
